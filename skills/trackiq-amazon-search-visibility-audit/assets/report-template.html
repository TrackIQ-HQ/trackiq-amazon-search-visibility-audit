What these numbers are, and what they are not

Read before quoting any figure above.

The gap is an estimate, not lost revenue

Gap revenue is query purchases × scale × the ASIN's average selling price, scaled from {{WEEKS_OBSERVED}} observed weeks to a {{PERIOD_DAYS}}-day month. It is the revenue currently transacting on queries where you are absent from the tracked set — not money you would certainly capture. Some of it already reaches you through other queries.

Share of voice is of tracked queries, not of the category

It is your impressions as a share of market impressions across the revenue-bearing queries measured here. It is not market share and should not be quoted as one.

Rank is ours only, read on one day

The data shows where your ASINs ranked on {{RANK_DATE}}. It does not show the results page, so this report never states which competitor holds a position. Untracked queries are reported as untracked, never as not ranking.
